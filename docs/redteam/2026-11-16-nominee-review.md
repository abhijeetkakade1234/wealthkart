# Nominee (backup) wallet: self-review (2026-11-16)

Review of `wt/nominee` (ADR 0016, built per [ADR 0056](../decisions/0056-nominee-wallet-built.md)) by the author with the `solidity-hardening` and `redteam-attack` skills, before merging. It is not independent; the external audit should look at it again (`docs/audit/README.md` lists what to challenge). Scope: `Exits.sol` (nominee, claim, `wake`, `_pay`, `_cancelClaim`, `_enqueue`), the `Kind.Claim` changes in `Membership.sol`, the vault entry points and `_afterVote`, and the app flows that send these transactions. Scenarios: [`../testing.md`](../testing.md) S-NOM-\*, I-14, S-E2E-50/51.

## What the attacker wants

- **Take a living member's share**: get them marked dormant, have the circle approve a claim, collect before they notice.
- **Redirect a payout** to a wallet the member never chose.
- **Break the exit queue** (double entry, lost entries, wrong `exitsWaiting`) to stall or skip exits, or pay a share twice.
- **Spam votes** or dodge the quorum rules.

## Hardening checklist (diff only)

- CEI: `_pay` books everything (unlink, totals, remove, nominee cleared) before `Ledger.payOut`; `_cancelClaim`, `closeClaim`, `setNominee`, `requestClaim` make no external calls.
- Reentrancy: every new write that touches the queue holds the transient lock (RV-NOM-01); `vote`/`finalize` already did, and `_afterVote` runs inside them.
- One payout path: nominees are paid by `Ledger.payOut` with its gas caps; refused or frozen payouts go to `withdrawable[nominee]` (S-NOM-08, S-NOM-09).
- Access: only the member sets their nominee; only the stored nominee can ask; only the member can cancel. No admin, no owner.
- Inputs: own wallet and the vault refused as nominee; zero means "clear" and can never ask (`NotNominee`).
- Bounded: no loops added; one open claim per member; requests capped as before (ADR 0015, 0033).
- Casts via `SafeCast`; no `unchecked`; `voterCount - 1` only when the nominee is counted in it.
- Events on every change: `NomineeSet`, `NomineeClaimRequested`, `NomineeClaimApproved`, `NomineeClaimCancelled`, `NomineePaid` (plus `RequestClosed`, `ExitPaid`).
- Slither (CI config, fail on medium): 0 medium or high; the only new finding during the work (a shadowed name, `firstPeriod`) was renamed.
