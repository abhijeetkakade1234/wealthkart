How to use it

  • Big flat colour blocks for the few things that matter: balance (cobalt), what you owe (mustard), the main action (red).
  • Cut-paper shapes (leaf, triangle, sun) only on hero tiles, empty states and the landing page. Never inside tables or forms.
  • Uneven corners everywhere instead of one fixed radius; 2px ink borders instead of shadows.
  • Member avatars are generated cut-paper shapes (triangle, sun, leaf) coloured from the wallet address.
  • Night mode: deep cobalt paper, cream text, mustard as the brand colour.

If this is the one, it becomes the shadcn theme (colours, corner shapes, borders, fonts) plus a small library of cut-paper shapes for decoration and avatars.
